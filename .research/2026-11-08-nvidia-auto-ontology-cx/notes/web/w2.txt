Overview
The Data Foundation for Agentic AI
To transform your enterprise, AI agents need continuous access to your data, putting strain on data infrastructure not designed for agentic reasoning loops.
With NVIDIA infrastructure, enterprises can meet the new volume and velocity of data demands from AI while leveraging their existing data stack. For CPU-based data processing, including analytics, real-time streaming, batch, and online transaction processing (OLTP), NVIDIA Vera delivers the per-core performance and high-bandwidth memory that agentic workloads need. For the fastest speed at scale, NVIDIA CUDA-X™ libraries accelerate the most popular data engines on GPUs, providing agents with on-demand access to structured data in tables and unstructured data living as PDFs, emails, images, and videos across the enterprise.
