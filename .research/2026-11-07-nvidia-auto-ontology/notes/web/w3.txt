Prune semantic FKs from empty or disjoint source columns (#401)

An inferred semantic FK is a guess from column names and descriptions.
Right after the edges are built, and before each group's join default is
chosen, check each one against the data and delete it when the source
column shares no value with its target, or holds no value at all.
Joining through either returns no rows, yet it was still offered as a
join path.

No overlap at all is the only test: real references in loaded data can
miss most of their target, so a low share is no proof.

Taken from a453e7b and e2181ec on fix/text-to-sql-message-order without
their role links and SQLite FK changes. On BEAVER dw it removed 17 of
137 edges (10 disjoint, 7 empty); MySQL confirms each, and none is a join
in the 300 gold queries.
