Reliable Agents: More Accurate, Grounded Answers
NVIDIA Nemotron Retriever
Open, leaderboard-winning models that improve agent context and lower agentic retrieval by 40%. Nemotron Parse processes multimodal documents up to 3.5x faster; Nemotron Embed produces 24% fewer incorrect answers; Nemotron Rerank improves retrieval accuracy by approximately 5%.
Access Nemotron Models
NVIDIA Kumo
Open, pretrained foundation models for making predictions with leading accuracy directly from relational, tabular, and time-series data. Kumo gives agents predictive context from structured data, enabling them to forecast outcomes, rank opportunities, recommend actions, and assess risk.
Access Kumo Models
NVIDIA Auto Ontology
Generates a starting ontology from enterprise data and documentation that business experts can test, refine, and certify. It complements existing ontology platforms and reduced ontology creation time by 90% in NVIDIA testing.
Get Started With Auto Ontology
More Performant Agents: Faster Access to Knowledge and Insights
NVIDIA cuDF
Accelerates the loading, joining, filtering, and transformation of structured data used by agents through familiar tools such as pandas, Polars, Spark, and DuckDB—by up to 5x on supported workloads.
Get Started With cuDF
NVIDIA cuML
Accelerates familiar classification, regression, clustering, and anomaly-detection algorithms for custom machine-learning pipelines—by up to 50x on supported workloads.
Get Started With cuML
NVIDIA cuOpt
Turns predictions, objectives and business constraints into optimized routes, schedules, and resource allocations—up to 10× faster on supported optimization workloads.
Get Started With cuOpt
NVIDIA cuVS
Accelerates the vector indexing and similarity search used to retrieve context for agents, with integrations for FAISS, Milvus, and OpenSearch—by up to 18x on supported workloads.
Get Started With cuVS
NVIDIA cuGraph
Accelerates analysis of relationships across customers, products, transactions, and other connected enterprise data, giving agents additional context for analysis and reasoning.

From Company Data to Company Intelligence:
Prepare: Use Nemotron Parse to preserve document content and structure, and cuDF to prepare structured data.
Retrievel: Use Nemotron Embed and cuVS to find relevant information, then Nemotron Rerank to prioritize the best evidence..
Understanding: Use Auto-Ontology to generate a starting ontology that business experts can test, refine, and certify.
Predict: Use cuGraph to analyze connected data, cuML for custom machine-learning pipelines, and Kumo for pretrained prediction over structured data.
Optimize: Use cuOpt to turn predictions, objectives, and constraints into optimized routes, schedules, and allocations.
