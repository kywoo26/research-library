 Submit a Change Form 
 You can submit your proposal in two ways: You can download the official Change Form template and then send it as an attachment to the STEMG email address. You can also attach images and supporting files. As an alternative, you can complete the web form (only text).  Note: File uploads are not supported in the web form. The STEMG reviews all Change Forms and incorporates agreed updates into the next issue of ASD-STE100.

 White Paper: Simplified Technical English and Artificial Intelligence 
 Artificial Intelligence is rapidly changing how technical content is created, translated, and managed. For the ASD-STE100 community, this brings both opportunities and risks.
 AI-generated text can appear clear, authoritative, and consistent with STE, even when it does not correctly apply the rules and vocabulary of the standard. Plausibility must not be confused with verified compliance.
 The new STEMG White Paper examines the responsible use of AI in STE environments, including human oversight, accountability, confidentiality, quality assurance, and the need to keep ASD-STE100 as the primary reference.
 AI can support technical authors, but it cannot replace professional expertise and informed review.
